## Pushing to main

**Changes go to `main` in batches, not one feature at a time.** Finish several changes first, then push them together.

- Never push or merge to `main` without a clear yes from the user. That yes covers that one push only.
- Don't ask after every feature. Keep finished, QA-passed work on its branch until the user says the batch is ready.
- This includes small changes and `TASKS.md` claim rows and row removals.

This overrides the steps in AGENTS.md that say to push a claim to `main` directly or to merge as soon as QA passes.
