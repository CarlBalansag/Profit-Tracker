# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Resellers running a small buy-and-resell business. They buy inventory from retail stores and online shops (and through cashout services that pay out per item), then sell it on marketplaces and to buyers. They use Profit Tracker on a phone while in a store or on the move, and at a desk for bookkeeping and review. It started as one reseller's own tool and is meant for other resellers too.

## Product Purpose

Replace the reseller's spreadsheet: log a purchase, record a sale, and see true per-item profit after purchase cost, tax, shipping both ways, marketplace fees and credit-card cashback. Success is knowing, at any moment, what needs doing next, where the money is, and what the business actually earned.

## Positioning

Profit is calculated per item with the card cashback that paid for it, across stores, cashout services and marketplaces, and the app turns the open work (items to ship, tracking to add, payments to chase, bills due) into one to-do list.

## Operating Context

- In a store: quick logging of a buy, checking what's owed or waiting, clearing a to-do, one-handed on a phone.
- At a desk: transactions history, cash flow, credit-card bills and cashback, analytics and goals, expenses, receipts, invoices, tax-exempt purchases and a Schedule C worksheet.
- Automation brings work in on its own: store order emails, cashout-service syncs and deal alerts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Web app (React + Vite), installable as a PWA; dark and light themes.
- Below 1024px the phone layout uses a bottom bar with a More drawer; from 1024px a sidebar is always visible.
- Main areas: Dashboard, To-Do, Calendar, Add Transaction, Record Sale, Transactions, Inventory, Email Automation, Shipping, Statuses, Cash Flow, Credit Card, Cashout (only for users with a cashout service), Analytics, Goals, Expenses, Receipts, Invoices, Tax Exempt, Schedule C (feature-flagged), Settings, Data Setup, Activity, Guide, What's New.
- A guided tour points at sidebar items.
- AI assistants can use the app through MCP tools.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Profit Tracker. No visual element is fixed for the navigation; the user allowed a new look for it.

## Evidence on Hand

Real user data exists in the app; no testimonials, customer counts or benchmarks are on hand and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. What needs doing comes first: open work is never more than one tap away.
2. Money shown is money that reconciles: numbers agree across every screen.
3. Built for one hand in a store as much as for a desk.
4. Fast paths for frequent jobs (log a buy, record a sale, clear a to-do); rare jobs stay findable without crowding.
5. Nothing hidden: the user always knows where they are and what's waiting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch targets at least 44px on touch screens, 16px text inputs on phones, keyboard and screen-reader support, WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
