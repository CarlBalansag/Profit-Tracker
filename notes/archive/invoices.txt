# INVOICES - DATABASE & LOGIC MAPPING

Invoices handle scenarios where money is owed to the user but hasn't been collected yet (usually B2B tech/wholesale or direct platform payouts).

## 1. TABLE SCHEMA
TABLE: `invoices`
- `id` (UUID, Primary Key)
- `user_id` (UUID, Foreign Key)
- `buyer_id` (UUID, Foreign Key) -> The entity who owes the money.
- `amount_due` (Decimal)
- `amount_paid` (Decimal)
- `status` (Enum: DRAFT, SENT, PARTIAL, PAID, OVERDUE)
- `due_date` (DateTime)
- `created_at` (DateTime)

## 2. LINKING TO SALES
- An invoice usually wraps up multiple `sales` rows into one bill.
- Requires a pivot table: `invoice_sales` (invoice_id, sale_id).
- **Dashboard Impact**: The `amount_due` minus `amount_paid` calculates directly to the "Total Owed" box on the Cash Flow page.
