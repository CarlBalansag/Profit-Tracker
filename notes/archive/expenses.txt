# EXPENSES - DATABASE & LOGIC MAPPING

Expenses represent business overhead that cannot be directly attributed to a specific unit of inventory. (e.g., Buying shipping tape, a subscription to a repricer tool).

## 1. THE EXPENSES QUERY
- **DB Logic**: `SELECT * FROM expenses WHERE expense_date BETWEEN [Start] AND [End]`

## 2. TABLE SCHEMA
TABLE: `expenses`
- `id` (UUID, Primary Key)
- `user_id` (UUID, Foreign Key)
- `name` (String) -> e.g. "Bubble Mailers", "Bqool Subscription"
- `category` (Enum: Supplies, Software, Fees, Misc)
- `amount` (Decimal)
- `expense_date` (DateTime)
- `payment_method_id` (UUID, Nullable) -> Links to credit cards table to track spend toward SUBs (Sign Up Bonuses).
- `recurring` (Boolean) -> Flag for monthly hits.
- `created_at`, `updated_at`

## 3. DASHBOARD IMPACT
When the Dashboard calculates `Profit (Accounting)`, it usually pulls the pure margin from `Sales`. However, globally, true Net Income must factor in overhead. 
- The DB must support a global metric: `Net Profit = Gross Margin Profit - SUM(expenses.amount)`.
